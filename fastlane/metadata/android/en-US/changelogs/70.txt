Adding items is quicker: type "2 L milk" and Pantry fills in the amount and suggests a category (needs Pantry server 0.40.0).

More features:
• Sync notes with Markdown files in your Nextcloud
• Add photos already in your Nextcloud
• Estimated totals on lists
• Undo a finished shopping trip
• Templates that pick up new items from their lists

Also: fixes for note checklists and inventory list names, and 3 smaller ones.

Full notes: https://github.com/chenasraf/pantry-flutter/releases/tag/v0.40.0
