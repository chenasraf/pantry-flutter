Adding items is quicker. Type "2 L milk" and Pantry fills in the amount and suggests a category,
based on what your household picked for that item before. Each guess shows as a chip you can tap to
drop, and anything you type or pick yourself always wins. Turn it off with "Smart input" in
Settings. Category and amount suggestions need the Pantry server app 0.40.0 or newer.
