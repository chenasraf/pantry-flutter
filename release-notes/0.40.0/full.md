## Highlights

### Items

- Adding items is quicker. Type "2 L milk" and Pantry fills in the amount and suggests a category,
  based on what your household picked for that item before. Each guess shows as a chip you can tap
  to drop, and anything you type or pick yourself always wins. Turn it off with "Smart input" in
  Settings. Category and amount suggestions need the Pantry server app 0.40.0 or newer. (mobile,
  desktop)

## New

### Lists

- Lists add up the prices of the items you still need to buy. Filter or search and the estimate
  follows along, so you can see what one project or one store run will cost before you head out.
  (mobile, desktop)
- Items sold at several stores no longer crowd their row with store chips. One chip shows how many
  stores carry the item, and tapping it lists them. (mobile, desktop)

### Notes

- Link a note to a Markdown file in your Nextcloud: pick a folder from the note's menu, and edits
  made to either one show up in the other. You can also turn a text file already in your Nextcloud
  into a note with Import from Nextcloud. (mobile, desktop)
- When a note is linked to a file in someone else's Nextcloud, the note shows whose files it's in,
  like "@Alice/Notes/Plants.md", so you don't go looking for it in your own. (mobile, desktop)

### Photos

- Add pictures that are already in your Nextcloud to items and the photo board, no re-uploading
  needed. Link to the file and it keeps showing even after you rename or move it, or import a copy
  that stays the same whatever happens to the original. (mobile, desktop)

### Shopping

- Finished a shopping trip by mistake? Undo it from shopping history: everything you checked off
  goes back on its list, and what the trip added to your inventories is taken back out. Anything
  that changed since the trip is left alone, and Pantry tells you which items those were. (mobile,
  desktop)

### Templates

- A template can now grow on its own: turn on "Automatically add new items from attached lists" in
  its settings, and anything new you add to those lists is copied into the template. Items a
  template already has are left as they are, so your own changes are never overwritten. (mobile,
  desktop)

## Fixes

### Inventory

- Inventory lists show their names, instead of a number, when you manage categories. (mobile,
  desktop)
- Inventory lists show their names, instead of a number, when you manage labels and custom fields.
  (mobile, desktop)
- Setting a category or labels on several inventory items at once offers the ones from their lists.
  (mobile, desktop)
- Inventory rows show at their full height. (desktop)

### Lists

- The templates you pick when creating a list, and the lists you pick when creating a template, are
  now saved. A new list also opens straight away instead of briefly showing an error. (mobile,
  desktop)
- Swiping over a row's open actions closes them. (mobile)

### Notes

- Checking off checklist items in a note works straight away, without opening the note for editing.
  (mobile, desktop)
